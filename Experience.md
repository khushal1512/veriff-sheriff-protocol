# Experience.md

## Our Journey at the MLH Hackathon

This is our first time participating in an MLH hackathon, and it has been nothing short of transformative. As a team of four passionate developers, we were excited to dive into the world of blockchain—a domain entirely new to all of us. The learning curve was steep: from understanding decentralized systems to mastering a new programming language and navigating the intricacies of the Midnight platform.

The Midnight ecosystem, while powerful and privacy-focused, presented significant challenges in setup, tooling, and documentation. As we began building, two of our teammates, overwhelmed by the complexity and time constraints, made the difficult decision to step away. But rather than give up, the remaining two of us doubled down—driven by a shared belief in our idea and a determination to deliver something meaningful.

We spent sleepless nights debugging, learning, and iterating. We wrestled with unfamiliar syntax, wrestled with local node configurations, and struggled to grasp zero-knowledge concepts at first. But with each obstacle, we grew stronger, more resilient, and more capable. We leaned on community forums, late-night Discord messages, and relentless Googling to piece together a working foundation.

And then—it clicked.

We built VSP (Veriff Sheriff Protocol): a privacy-preserving user-attestation system on the Midnight Blockchain. VSP enables secure verification of sensitive user attributes—like government IDs, vaccination records, facial biometrics, and financial credibility—without ever exposing that data on-chain. Inspired by the principle of "Verify Once, Use Anywhere", our system allows trusted verifiers to issue attestations that users can selectively share across applications, all while preserving anonymity and consent.

This project is more than a hack—it’s a vision. We believe privacy should be a default, not a luxury. With VSP, we’re laying the groundwork for a future where individuals control their identity, and organizations can verify claims securely and efficiently.

Though we’re proud of what we’ve built in such a short time, we know this is just the beginning. Our next step is to refine VSP into a full-fledged open-source SDK, enabling developers everywhere to integrate secure, privacy-first attestation into their dApps with ease.

We came into this hackathon as beginners. We’re leaving as builders—with a working prototype, a clear mission, and the fire to keep going.

Thank you, MLH, for this incredible opportunity. We can’t wait to see where VSP goes next.

— The VSP Team
